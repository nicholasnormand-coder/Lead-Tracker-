Nicholas Normand, REALTOR®

## Layers

### Nick (Owner / Broker Agent)
The only human in the chart. Owns the license, the relationships, and every decision that touches a client or a contract. Nothing is signed, sent, or committed without him.

### ChatGPT CEO + Chief of Staff
Takes Nick's goals and turns them into assignments. Decides what gets worked on, in what order, and by which arm. Reports back in plain language: what got done, what's stuck, what needs a human.

### Grok Workforce
The staff. Five departments, each with its own agents, each with one job.

- **COO / Operations**: moves deals from contract to close. Transactions, buyers, listings, contract deadlines, and LREC compliance.
- **CGO / Growth**: fills the pipeline. Captures leads, qualifies them, follows up, nurtures the slow ones, and hunts for opportunities nobody sent in.
- **CMO / Marketing**: gets listings seen. Listing copy, social posts, Marketplace, and a compliance check on everything before it goes out.
- **CFO / BI**: keeps score. Commission tracking, expenses, return on each lead source, and forecasting.
- **Cenla Intelligence**: knows the market. Local market research, MLS data, financing, competitors, and investment analysis for Central Louisiana.

### Argus Command Center
The dashboard. One place to see the pipeline, deadlines, money, and every agent's status.

### Claude Code Engineering
Builds and maintains the tools the rest of the system runs on, starting with this repo (the Lead Tracker).

## Rules

- Agents draft. Nick sends and signs.
- Everything client-facing passes Brand Compliance and LREC / NAR / Fair Housing checks.
- Every department reports up through the CEO layer, never straight to Nick.
